Oplenac Folklore Group rules and regulations

For dancers and parents

  1. It is the obligation of all the parents to make sure their children treat the church property with respect and not cause any damage to the church grounds. No fighting, swearing, property damage, or disrespect towards a teacher and others will be tolerated. Violations can lead to exclusion for the dancer.
  2. Any damage made to the church or injuries by the dancer will be the sole responsibility of the parent. Violations can lead to exclusion for the dancer.
  3. Dance uniforms are the responsibility of the dancer. If any part of the uniform is damaged or lost, the dancer/parent will be liable for the replacement cost.
  4. Parents are expected to volunteer in the kitchen 3 Fridays during the year. If parents cannot work on their scheduled day, it is THEIR responsibility to notify the Board and find their own replacement. The Board will do its best to remind you of your upcoming kitchen schedule, but it is ultimately your responsibility to put it on your calendar and set up your reminders.
  5. Parents are expected to volunteer their time at dances/festivals. Our dances are fundraisers for our own group and every family MUST help at each dance. These events are announced with ample time to ensure your availability.
  6. If parents do not volunteer at dances and Friday night practices, their dancer will not be eligible to receive benefits — their expenses to participate in our group activity may not be covered by the group (travel and hotel costs for group trips).
  7. There will be no congregating upstairs during practice. This includes children who are not practicing, as well as parents. NO FOOD OR DRINKS (EXCEPT WATER) UPSTAIRS.
  8. These rules are set in place for the safety and security of our children. Oplenac is a voluntary organization that relies solely on the help of our members. By signing this form, you and your dancer agree to abide by the rules and regulations above to ensure that we have another successful year.

You must agree to the Rules and Regulations to register.
